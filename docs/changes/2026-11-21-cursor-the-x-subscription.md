# Cursor moves to The X Subscription (Plus $8, Premium $30, Super $100, Ultra $200)

**Date:** 21 November 2026 · **Tool:** Cursor · **Verified:** 21 November 2026

## What changed

**Before:** Individual plans Hobby (free), Pro at $20/month, Pro+ at $60 and Ultra at $200, each with two monthly usage pools and optional on-demand usage billed in arrears.

**After:** A new plan family, **The X Subscription**:

| Plan | Price | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Plus | $8/mo | Base weekly pool |
| Premium | $30/mo | 4× Plus |
| Super | $100/mo | 15× Plus |
| Ultra | $200/mo | 50× Plus |

The new plans use **one weekly usage pool** for Cursor models, shared with Grok. Legacy plan holders keep their old plans, prices and features unless they switch.
