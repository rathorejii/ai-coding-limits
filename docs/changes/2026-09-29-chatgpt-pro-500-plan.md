# ChatGPT Pro: a new $500 plan, Pro 200 reopens with a lower allowance

**Date:** 29 September 2026 · **Tool:** Codex (OpenAI) · **Verified:** 21 November 2026

## What changed

**Before:** Pro was sold as a $100 tier and a $200 tier, described as 5× or 20× more Codex usage than Plus, with five-hour message estimates listed for both. New sign-ups and upgrades to the $200 plan had been paused since 10 September 2026.

**After:** Three Pro tiers are on sale — **Pro 100 ($100), Pro 200 ($200) and Pro 500 ($500)**:

- Only Pro 500 includes Astra Ultrafast
- New Pro 200 subscriptions that are not eligible for grandfathering get a **lower usage allowance** than the original tier; eligible existing subscriptions move to it after 29 October 2026
- Pro plans currently have **no five-hour limit**; weekly limits may also apply
