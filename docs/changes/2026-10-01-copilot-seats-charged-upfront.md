# Copilot Business and Enterprise seats are charged upfront for credit card and PayPal customers

**Date:** 1 October 2026 · **Tool:** GitHub Copilot · **Verified:** 21 November 2026

## What changed

**Before:** Seats were billed each month for the number assigned, with billed users counted at the end of each billing cycle. Credit card and PayPal payers paid upfront only for seats added during a cycle, prorated for the rest of that cycle.

**After:** For credit card and PayPal payers, **all assigned seats are charged upfront** at the start of each billing cycle, and each new seat is paid for before its user gets access. Seats added mid-cycle are still prorated, and included usage may be prorated to match.

Prices are unchanged: **Business $19** and **Enterprise $39** per user per month.
