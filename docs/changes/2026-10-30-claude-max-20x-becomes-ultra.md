# Claude Max 20x becomes Ultra, and Max 5x becomes Max

**Date:** 30 October 2026 · **Tool:** Claude (Anthropic) · **Verified:** 21 November 2026

## What changed

**Before:** Two Max tiers — Max 5x at $100/month and Max 20x at $200/month, at five and twenty times the Pro plan's per-session usage allowance.

**After:** One **Max** plan at $100/month (5× Pro usage every five hours and every week) and a new top tier, **Ultra**, at $200/month:

- 10× the Pro plan's weekly usage
- **No five-hour limit for verified accounts**
- A $200 monthly API credit included

Anyone searching for "Claude Max 20x" today is looking at a plan that no longer exists under that name — the $200 tier is now Ultra.
